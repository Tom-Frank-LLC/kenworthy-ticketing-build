The rule underneath this: text color follows the background's lightness, not its hue. The marquee-bright greens (≈50% lightness) are light enough that they need dark text — white on them fails. If the team wants a green that carries white text (to match how the magenta/purple buttons work), it has to be a dark green (≈22–28% lightness) like Forest/Pine/Bottle above. So the real question isn't "white or black text" — it's which green: a bright heritage accent that always pairs with black text, or a deep forest green that pairs with white. Both are valid; they just feel different (bright botanical vs. deep velvet-curtain).
