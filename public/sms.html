Text message terms

Kenworthy Performing Arts Centre · 508 S Main St, Moscow, ID 83843

We can text you your tickets. This page explains exactly what that means: what we send, how often, how you say yes, and how you stop. Nothing here is a summary of a longer document — this is the whole programme.

What we send

Ticket confirmations and updates about an order you have placed. A text carries the show, the date and time, your seats if the showing has them, and a link to your tickets. That is the entire message type.

We do not send marketing, promotions, fundraising appeals or newsletters by text. Those go by email, and only to people who ask for them separately.

How you opt in

On the ticket checkout form at the Kenworthy website, the phone field is optional, and next to it is a checkbox that is unchecked by default. Texts are sent only if you tick it.

You never have to agree to texts in order to buy a ticket. Consent is not a condition of purchase, of entry, or of anything else. Leave the box alone and your tickets arrive by email as they always have.

This is the checkbox, word for word as it appears at checkout:

Text me my tickets. I agree to receive ticket confirmations and updates about this order by text message from the Kenworthy Performing Arts Centre at the number above. Message frequency varies — usually one message per order. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. Optional — your tickets come by email either way.

How often

Message frequency varies — usually one message per order. Texts are tied to a purchase, so you hear from us when you buy tickets and not otherwise. There is no recurring schedule and no campaign you are added to.

Cost

Msg & data rates may apply. We do not charge for texts; your mobile carrier may, according to your own plan.

How to stop

Reply STOP to cancel. You will get one confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and then nothing further. You can also reply UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END or QUIT.

Reply HELP for help, and you will get our contact details back. You can also reach us the ordinary ways, listed at the bottom of this page. If you ever want texts again, reply START.

Opting out of texts does not cancel your tickets and does not affect your email confirmations.

Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. A text is a convenience; your ticket is the emailed confirmation and the QR code it carries, which is what the box office scans at the door.

Your number

A number you give us is used to send you the messages described above and for nothing else. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
